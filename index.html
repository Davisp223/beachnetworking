Services

What we do

Consulting first. Every job starts with understanding what you have and what you need, then a plan you can actually follow.

Network overhauls

Replacing a network that grew without a plan. A clean redesign and rebuild, start to finish:

  • Topology and IP addressing redesign
  • VLANs and segmentation
  • Switching, routing and firewall setup
  • Cabling and rack cleanup

Network assessments

A walkthrough of your current setup with a written report: what's working, what's at risk, and what to fix first.

Security hardening

Firewall rules, secure remote access, guest and IoT isolation, and closing the gaps that default settings leave open.

Wi-Fi design

Access point placement and configuration for full coverage, with no dead zones and no slowdowns when everyone is online.

Small business IT setup

New office or new location? Internet, network gear, Wi-Fi, printers and the basics, set up properly from day one.

Documentation

Network diagrams, device inventories and configuration records, so the next person who touches your network knows what's there.

Process

How a project works

  1. Consult

    A conversation about your goals, your budget and the problems you're running into.

  2. Assess

    A look at your current equipment, layout and configuration.

  3. Plan

    A written proposal with recommendations, equipment and cost before any work begins.

  4. Build & hand off

    The work gets done, tested and documented, and you get the documentation.
